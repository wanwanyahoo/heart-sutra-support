Frequently asked questions

Is reading required to end a quiet window?

No. Reading and the window are independent. Completing a reading does not shorten protection. You can always end early for free from the quiet-window screen or clear protection in Settings. Selected apps should become available when the window ends. If authorization is revoked, device settings and the iOS system determine protection.

Do I need Family Sharing or a child account?

No. Family Controls is Apple’s Screen Time framework. This app requests individual authorization to help you manage your own distractions. App selections are represented by opaque system tokens.

What is free, and what does Plus add?

Bundled scripture, reader settings, notes, bookmarks, quiet windows, early ending, backup import/export, all journal records, two saved practices and a seven-day overview are free. Plus is an optional one-time purchase adding up to eight reusable saved practices and a 28-day overview. There is no subscription. The exact local price appears on Apple’s purchase sheet.

How do I restore Plus?

Open Settings → Plus → Restore purchases using the Apple account that purchased it. Internet access and Apple services may be needed. Pending or cancelled purchases do not unlock Plus. Refunds can revoke paid access; your private records remain readable and exportable.

Which languages are supported?

Interface: English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese and Thai. Interface language and scripture edition are separate. Each reading identifies the actual edition language, translator and version.

Can I back up or delete my records?

Use Settings → Export backup, Import backup or Delete local data. Exported files contain personal notes and reflections: store them privately. Backups do not contain Screen Time authorization, app tokens, active windows or purchase ownership. Delete exported copies separately.

How can I get help?

Open a support request. Requests are public. Include app version, iOS version and steps; do not include personal reflections, credentials, app selections or receipts. You may write in any supported language.
