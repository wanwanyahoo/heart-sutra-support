Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026. This policy covers Heart Sutra (org.selah.muyuretreat).

Local records

Reading history, bookmarks, preferences, personal notes, saved practices, quiet intentions and reflections are stored on your device. The app has no app account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated synchronization server. The developer does not receive your records, selected apps or browsing history through this app.

Apple Screen Time manages selections using opaque tokens. An app-group container lets this app and its protection extensions coordinate locally. Individual authorization uses Apple’s system interface and can be revoked in iOS Settings. No selected-app names or browsing activity are sent to a developer server.

Purchases and reminders

Apple processes optional one-time Plus purchases. StoreKit verifies purchase ownership and revocation on your device. The developer does not receive payment-card details. Apple’s policies apply to payment, purchase history, refunds and other Apple services. Optional reminders are local notifications and require permission; no developer push server is used.

Sharing, backups and deletion

Sharing and backup export happen only when you choose them. Backups include personal records, notes and saved practices. The Files destination or cloud provider you select governs any resulting transfer. Exports exclude Screen Time authorization, app-selection tokens, active protection and purchase ownership. You can delete local practice data in Settings. Delete exported copies and device/cloud backups separately. Apple’s device backup settings remain under your control.

External websites and support

Source and publisher links open external websites and are governed by their privacy policies. These GitHub Pages support pages may generate technical access logs under the GitHub privacy statement. We add no analytics scripts, third-party fonts or tracking pixels.

GitHub support issues are public. Do not post personal notes, credentials, app selections or purchase receipts. Information you choose to submit is used to understand and respond to your request.

Changes

Updates will be published here with a new effective date. This policy does not replace the privacy terms of Apple or the external publishers you choose to visit.
