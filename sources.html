Published sources

Read Xuanzang’s Classical Chinese Heart Sutra transcription or F. Max Müller’s historical English translation, published by Oxford’s Clarendon Press in 1894. Each retains its own paragraph divisions and provenance. Named Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese and modern English publications are available through publisher-hosted online links, requiring internet access and subject to the publisher’s terms. Japanese is a recitation book. A verified Thai scripture translation is not bundled. Scripture is never generated or machine-translated.

Original source

Heart Sutra — F. Max Müller (1894)

F. Max Müller; Oxford, Clarendon Press · Sacred Books of the East XLIX, Part II (1894), pp. 153–154

Public domain: historical translation published in 1894; F. Max Müller died in 1900.

The Smaller Pragñâ-pâramitâ-hridaya-sûtra, translated by F. Max Müller, in Buddhist Mahâyâna Texts, Sacred Books of the East XLIX, Part II, Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1894, pp. 153–154. Transcribed and visually checked against the printed pages in the Sakya University Library scan (PDF pages 184–185). Historical spelling and wording retained. Print line wraps are joined; typographic quotation marks are represented in Unicode. The printed footnote is retained separately. This is a historical scholarly English translation of the Sanskrit short recension, not a new translation or a claim of endorsement by a Buddhist institution. Its paragraphs are not aligned verse by verse with Xuanzang's Chinese recension.
